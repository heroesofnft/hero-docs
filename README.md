# What is Heroes of NFT?

**Heroes of NFT** is the project developing a turn-based collectible trading card game called [**Luminoria Tactics**](https://luminoriatactics.com) **(LUTA)**. In this game, players battle in the arena, showcasing their strategic skills with unique hero cards. The game offers endless possibilities, challenging competitors to become true strategists.

In-game items such as spell cards, weapons, and cosmetics significantly influence the pace of the game, while also providing opportunities for deeper customization. These items are wallet-ownable and tradable on the marketplace, adding value to the HON economy.

With future game modes offering varied challenges, players will face off in serious tournaments, where the winners will earn generous rewards. PvP and PvE modes are separately rewarded with mintable HRM tokens, with the most prestigious prizes reserved for tournament victors.

The game features novel Play-to-Earn mechanics, allowing players to participate in a real game economy. HON token holders will have a direct role in shaping the future of the game, becoming the true game masters.

With its innovative features, competitive gameplay, and rich content, **Heroes of NFT** distinguishes itself with **Luminoria Tactics (LUTA)**, a game that blends the best of blockchain technology and strategic depth.
